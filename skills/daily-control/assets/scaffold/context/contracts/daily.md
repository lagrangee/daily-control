# Daily Contract

One file per local day lives at `daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Ownership

- `open` owns the `## Open` section under the Skill's confirmation rules, including the stable `### Policy overrides` projection. Situation, Judgment, Direction, and Plan are optional; a useful interaction may finish without a write. Preserve existing formats and content.
- `refresh` owns the `## Evidence` section and preserves valid prior facts on a current source failure.
- `shutdown` owns the `## Shutdown` section after user confirmation.
- User-authored sections outside these headings are preserved.

Durable Daily writes and exact readback occur before `context/now.md` is updated.
