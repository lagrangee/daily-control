## Closeout and later feedback

This synthetic exchange illustrates a judgment-only day with incomplete Evidence. It is an example of conversation, not an additional record to copy into the snapshot.

```text
Earlier durable Direction: Try a shorter drafting session because the user
suspects session length is blocking progress; reconsider if it does not help.
Today's Open: The user chose to explore the problem without a delivery target.
Today's Evidence: Partial; reading observed, project coverage unavailable.

User: /daily-control shutdown
Agent: Draft: exploration remained today's direction. Saved Evidence records
       reading but cannot establish project progress or whether the shorter
       session helped. Is there an important result or correction to preserve?
User: I tried the shorter session. It did not help; I still cannot tell what
      counts as an acceptable draft. Leave that question open.
Agent: I will record your feedback and the unresolved acceptance question.

User, at a later Open: /daily-control open
Agent: The prior shorter-session experiment did not help, according to your
       Shutdown. Its premise may need revision: acceptance uncertainty could
       matter more than session length. Is that still the unresolved issue?
User: Yes. Today I want to understand the criteria, without a delivery promise.
Agent: I will record that direction and why it changed.
```

A weekly review may use that feedback in its existing Learning section. Any proposal to change long-term policy or Controller Style still needs separate user confirmation; the day's choice does not change those defaults.
